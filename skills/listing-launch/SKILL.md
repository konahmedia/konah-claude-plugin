# Listing launch

Line up everything a new listing needs in KONAH, as drafts the agent approves.

1. Find the property: `list_orders` with the street name (or `my_properties`).
   - No KONAH media order yet? Offer `order_draft` for photos/video. It never books or pays; the agent finishes it in KONAH.
2. Ask only what you don't know: list price is NOT needed and you never state one unless the agent gives it. Ask for 2-3 features they want to lead with.
3. Make the drafts:
   - `social_draft` for a Just Listed post in their voice (features, neighborhood, a clear call to action, no price unless they gave it).
   - `hello_draft` for a short KONAH Hello video page with a 30-second script they can read.
   - For their sphere or buyers: `find_contacts` or `smart_lists`, then `draft_email` to the few people they name. Keep each one personal and short.
4. Finish with a checklist of what was drafted and where: posts in AI Social (they approve there), emails in Ask KONAH → Ready to send, the Hello page in KONAH Hello to record.

Fair Housing: describe the home and the area's features, never who the home is "perfect for" by family status, religion, national origin, age, disability or similar. No promises about rates, approvals or offers.
