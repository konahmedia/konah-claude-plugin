# KONAH for Claude

KONAH is the CRM and listing-media studio for real estate agents. This plugin connects Claude to your KONAH account and adds six ready-made workflows:

| Skill | Say something like |
|---|---|
| good-morning-konah | "Good morning, what's my day?" |
| listing-launch | "123 Oak Dr just went live" |
| after-showing | "Just showed the Garcias two homes, log it" |
| weekly-pipeline-review | "Do my Monday pipeline review" |
| open-house-follow-up | "Add today's open house sign-ins" |
| sphere-touch | "Who in my sphere should I check in with?" |

## Connect
The plugin uses the KONAH connector at `https://konahmedia.com/api/mcp`. The first time, Claude opens the Connect KONAH page: sign in to KONAH and tap Allow.

## What it can and can't do
- It works only on your own KONAH account.
- Notes, tasks, stages, tags and drafts happen when you ask, and can be undone in KONAH → Ask KONAH → What KONAH did.
- Emails, texts and posts are **only drafted**. They wait in KONAH → Ask KONAH → Ready to send for your own Send.
- It never deletes anything, pays, or changes your plan, password or account.
- Disconnect any time in KONAH → Settings → Connected apps.

Needs a KONAH CRM 2.0 plan. Questions: support@konahmedia.com · https://konahmedia.com/crm
